Wake Up, Work on Dreams, Back to Bed and Lucid Dream: A Sleep Laboratory Study (Erlacher, D., & Stumbrys, T. 2020) 22 NOV

Waking participants after six hours to practice MILD for 30 to 60 minutes produced lucid dreams in 36 to 54% of unselected participants, compared with 9% after reading.

Vanilla Sky 22 NOV

A man whose life unravels after a disfiguring accident slowly realizes that the world he inhabits is a lucid dream.

Commentary on Konkoly et al. 2021 that discusses whether longer two-way conversations with lucid dreamers are feasible.

TritonSleep 22 NOV

Private on-device dream journal, reality-check reminders and guided MILD, WBTB, SSILD, WILD and FILD sessions with evidence ratings; no account.

The Effectiveness of Lucid Dreaming Therapy in Patients With Nightmares: A Systematic Review (Ouchene, R., et al. 2023) 22 NOV

Found 4 randomized controlled trials and several case studies; most reported fewer nightmares in adults, but internal validity was limited.

The Cognitive Neuroscience of Lucid Dreaming (Baird, B., Mota-Rolim, S. A., & Dresler, M. 2019) 22 NOV

Reviewed EEG, neuroimaging, lesion, drug and brain stimulation studies, finding EEG results mostly underpowered and mixed, prefrontal and parietal regions implicated, and cholinergic stimulation combined with mental set training the most promising induction approach.

The Clinical Neuroscience of Lucid Dreaming (Tzioridou, S., et al. 2025) 22 NOV

Reviewed links between lucid dreaming and nightmare disorder, depression, anxiety, psychosis and dissociation, and the potential drawbacks of lucid dreaming interventions.

Targeted Lucidity Reactivation Implemented in an Open-Source watchOS App (Mallela, R., & Mallett, R. 2024) 22 NOV

Developed an open-source iOS/watchOS application that runs targeted lucidity reactivation at home, using Apple Watch motion and heart rate data to time cues to REM sleep.

TLR 22 NOV

Open-source iOS/watchOS app for targeted lucidity reactivation, with Apple Watch sleep staging to time cues to REM sleep.

Superliminal 22 NOV

First-person puzzle game about escaping a surreal dream world by bending perspective.

Sleep and Dream Database 22 NOV

Kelly Bulkeley's archive of dream reports and survey data for research.

Reality Testing and the Mnemonic Induction of Lucid Dreams: Findings From the National Australian Lucid Dream Induction Study (Adventure-Heart, D. J., et al. 2017) 22 NOV

Compared reality testing, WBTB and MILD in 169 participants and found that combining all three induced lucid dreams, especially when participants fell asleep soon after practicing MILD.

Provoking Lucid Dreams at Home With Sensory Cues Paired With Pre-Sleep Cognitive Training (Konkoly, K. R., et al. 2024) 22 NOV

Moved targeted lucidity reactivation (TLR) to a smartphone without polysomnography. Lucid dreaming increased compared with the prior week and with blinded control nights, but only when the sleep cues matched the pre-sleep training sounds.

Pre-Sleep Treatment With Galantamine Stimulates Lucid Dreaming: A Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Crossover Study (LaBerge, S., LaMarca, K., & Baird, B. 2018) 22 NOV

In 121 participants combining WBTB and MILD, 4 mg and 8 mg of galantamine, a prescription drug, raised the share reporting a lucid dream to 27% and 42%, compared with 14% on placebo.

Paller Lab 22 NOV

Ken Paller's cognitive neuroscience lab at Northwestern University, behind the two-way dream communication and at-home TLR studies.

Oniri 22 NOV

Dream journal with voice recording, dream interpretation and lucid dreaming lessons.

Oniri 22 NOV

Neural Correlates of Dream Lucidity Obtained From Contrasting Lucid Versus Non-Lucid REM Sleep: A Combined EEG/fMRI Case Study (Dresler, M., et al. 2012) 22 NOV

In the one participant (of four) with lucid REM episodes long enough to analyze, lucid dreaming activated the precuneus, parietal lobules, and prefrontal and occipito-temporal cortices compared with non-lucid REM sleep.

Metacognitive Mechanisms Underlying Lucid Dreaming (Filevich, E., Dresler, M., et al. 2015) 22 NOV

Found greater frontopolar (BA9/10) gray matter volume in people reporting high dream lucidity, and stronger activation of the same region when they monitored their own thoughts while awake.

Measuring Consciousness in Dreams: The Lucidity and Consciousness in Dreams Scale (Voss, U., et al. 2013) 22 NOV

Developed the LuCiD scale, with eight factors including insight, control, thought, memory and dissociation, now a standard outcome measure in lucid dreaming studies.

Lucid Scribe 22 NOV

Dream journal, Homing Signal cue training, guided MILD, SSILD, WBTB and FILD, targeted lucidity reactivation, and REM detection with the INSPEC device.

Lucid Scribe 22 NOV

Dream journal, Homing Signal cue training, guided MILD, SSILD, WBTB and FILD, targeted lucidity reactivation, and REM detection with the INSPEC device.

Lucid Dreaming: Intensity, But Not Frequency, Is Inversely Related to Psychopathology (Aviram, L., & Soffer-Dudek, N. 2018) 22 NOV

In 187 students, lucid dream frequency was unrelated to psychopathology, while deliberate induction predicted increases in dissociation and schizotypy over two months.

Lucid Dreaming, Plain and Simple by Robert Waggoner and Caroline McCready 22 NOV

Short, practical introduction to lucid dreaming techniques and what to do once lucid.

Lucid Dreaming Treatment for Nightmares: A Pilot Study (Spoormaker, V. I., & van den Bout, J. 2006) 22 NOV

Randomized 23 nightmare sufferers to individual, group or waiting-list conditions; one 2-hour lucid dreaming treatment session reduced nightmare frequency at 12 weeks, but becoming lucid was not necessary for the improvement.

Lucid Dreaming Occurs in Activated Rapid Eye Movement Sleep, Not a Mixture of Sleep and Wakefulness (Baird, B., Tononi, G., & LaBerge, S. 2022) 22 NOV

Found that signal-verified lucid dreams occur during REM sleep with higher eye movement density, and that the reported 40 Hz "hybrid state" signal was an eye movement artifact.

Lucid Dreaming Incidence: A Quality Effects Meta-Analysis of 50 Years of Research (Saunders, D. T., et al. 2016) 22 NOV

Estimated that 55% of people have had at least one lucid dream and 23% have them monthly or more.

Lucid Dream 22 NOV

A journalist uses lucid dreaming to search his memories for clues to his son's kidnapping.

Llewellyn's Complete Book of Lucid Dreaming by Clare R. Johnson 22 NOV

Comprehensive guide to lucid dreaming techniques and to using lucid dreams for creativity, nightmares and sleep problems.

Institut für Bewusstseins- und Traumforschung 22 NOV

Brigitte Holzinger's institute in Vienna, offering research, seminars and lucid dreaming therapy for nightmares.

Induction of Self Awareness in Dreams Through Frontal Low Current Stimulation of Gamma Activity (Voss, U., et al. 2014) 22 NOV

Reported that 25 and 40 Hz transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS) during REM sleep induced self-reflective awareness in dreams, while other frequencies did not.

Inducing Lucid Dreaming With Multisensory Stimulation: A Preregistered Multi-Center Study (Picard-Deland, C., Jafarzadeh Esfahani, M., et al. 2026) 22 NOV

Preprint. Combined pre-sleep SSILD training with light, sound and vibration cues during REM sleep in 60 participants across three labs. Cueing did not make lucid dreams more frequent than sham, but roughly doubled the duration of signal-verified lucid dreams.

IASD Annual Conference 22 NOV

Yearly conference of the International Association for the Study of Dreams, with research talks and lucid dreaming workshops.

Frequent Lucid Dreaming Associated With Increased Functional Connectivity Between Frontopolar Cortex and Temporoparietal Association Areas (Baird, B., et al. 2018) 22 NOV

Found stronger resting-state connectivity between the anterior prefrontal cortex and temporoparietal regions in frequent lucid dreamers, but no differences in brain structure.
