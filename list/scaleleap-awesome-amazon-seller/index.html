Amazon Ads scheduling and automation for Seller Central and Vendor Central accounts in all marketplaces across the North America, Europe and Far East regions. Runs dayparting, budget, event, performance and bid-adjustment rules on schedules and conditions the seller defines, checked every 15 minutes. Flat $149 per month per Amazon Ads account, never a percentage of ad spend. 14-day free trial.

Firecrawl 27 OCT

Web scraping and search API for Amazon data. Scrape public Amazon pages as markdown (tested on Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk and Amazon.de), or use structured data providers for Amazon.com (product details, prices, buy-box offers, reviews, search), Amazon.com.br and Amazon.nl.

Vendooly 25 OCT

MCP connector that brings Seller Central and Amazon Ads into ChatGPT and Claude through the official Amazon APIs. The AI prepares each change with a before/after preview and it runs only after your approval. 14-day free trial.

VNCNT Labs Keyword Research 25 OCT

Reverse ASIN (the Amazon keywords any ASIN ranks for) and keyword ideas from a seed via Amazon related searches and Google autocomplete, with search volume, CPC, difficulty and a USPTO trademark flag. Runs on Apify, pay per result from $0.002 per keyword, no subscription.

Utlista 25 OCT

Localises an existing product catalogue into Amazon listings for seven EU marketplaces (de, fr, it, es, nl, pl, se). Writes title, five bullets, description and backend search terms per destination marketplace rather than translating them, and validates every field against Amazon's limits before export, including search terms counted in bytes. Produces a flat file per marketplace; it does not upload to Amazon. Free for ten products.

ScrapeUnblocker 25 OCT

Web scraping API with Amazon endpoints that return JSON: product pages (price, list price, availability, rating, seller, images), search results with sponsored and Prime flags, Best Sellers and other ranked lists, and review data with the star histogram, across Amazon's regional marketplaces. Also available as an MCP server.

RefVid 25 OCT

AI product video generator that keeps label, logo and packaging consistent across clips, from 1-5 photos or an Amazon product link. Free credits on sign-up.

ChineseCheck 25 OCT

Verify a Chinese supplier before paying: look up the company by English name, Chinese name or Unified Social Credit Code and read its registration record in English for free; paid reports add court enforcement, litigation and penalty records.

zonfy Free Seller Tools 16 OCT

Paid AI A+ Content and listing generator.

ZonRival 13 OCT

Amazon competitor research inside Claude and ChatGPT via MCP: review complaint topics from Amazon's "Customers say" summaries, 90-day price and rank history, listing audits, keyword rankings with advertisers, and daily competitor alerts. Free review complaint checker, no sign-up.

Vendlen 13 OCT

Amazon Ads analytics and automation for sellers on the US marketplace. Each recommendation shows the evidence, period and scope behind it; automation rules run inside limits you set and can be previewed before they change anything. 14-day free trial.

Listimo 13 OCT

AI listing checker (score 0-100) and generator for Amazon titles, bullet points, A+ content, listing images and video from one product photo. Free daily check without signup; credits instead of a subscription.

B2B Supplier Hub 13 OCT

Supplier discovery for Amazon wholesale sellers and resellers. Search by brand, product or UPC to see which distributors carry an item, wholesale cost ranges, stock levels and last-updated dates; pay only to reveal a distributor. Chrome extension shows distributors behind Amazon listings. Free plan.

Sellantica 4 OCT

Amazon PPC automation for sellers and agencies: one-click campaign structures, keyword harvesting from auto campaigns, bid optimization with explainable reasoning, and per-product P&L. EU-hosted.

AdsTurbo 28 SEP

ListCraft HQ 18 SEP

Free, no-signup suite of calculators for sellers covering profit margin, markup, break-even, ROAS, CAC, and LTV. No login or paywall.
