Additions

Sharp error tolerance of the monogamy-of-entanglement game (2026) 11 NOV

Improved loss tolerance of f-measure in the random oracle model and in the plain model under parallel repetition, enabled by an improved analysis of the monogamy-of-entanglement game

Secure quantum ranging (2025) 11 NOV

Proposes a secure quantum ranging protocol that combines quantum ranging with quantum position verification.

Robust logarithmic entanglement lower bound for $f$-routing (2026) 11 NOV

Proves robust logarithmic lower bound for $f$-route with the binary inner product function

Relativistic position verification with coherent states (2026) 11 NOV

Experimental implementation of the $f$-measure protocol using phase-randomized weak coherent states

Quantum position verification in one shot: parallel repetition of the $f$-BB84 and $f$-routing protocols (2025) 11 NOV

Proves security of $f$-route and $f$-measure under parallel repetition for random functions $f$.

Quantum gate lower bounds for loss-tolerant position verification 11 NOV

Proves nearly-linear quantum gate lower for $f$-measure, even when allowing the attacker to declare a transmission loss of up to 50%. Proof is analytic, improved analysis of the monogamy-of-entanglement game.

Quantum Position Verification with Remote Untrusted Devices (2026) 11 NOV

Introduces and proves device-independent security of a Bell-test based QPV protocol which resembles $f$-measure, implements the protocol in an experiment

Private Proofs of When and Where (2026) 11 NOV

Introduces zero-knowledge position verification and constructs such protocols from secure position verification and post-quantum one-way functions.

Perfect non-local quantum computation is impossible(2026) 11 NOV

Proves that perfect NLQC is impossible.

Perfect cheating is impossible for single-qubit position verification (2026) 11 NOV

Considers a generalisation where the input qubits are eigenstates of a $\mathbb{C}^2$ projector chosen uniformly at random. Shows that no finite-dimensional resource state can perfectly attack this protocol.

Orthogonality Broadcasting and Quantum Position Verification (2025) 11 NOV

Studies the difference between unentangled quantum and classical attacks on state discrimination QPV protocols and proves several results on that, for example that there is a separable state ensemble that can be perfectly discriminated with quantum communication, but not without.

On the role of quantum communication and loss in attacks on quantum position verification (2022) 11 NOV

Shows that any QPV protocol that can be broken unentangled with quantum communication, but is secure against classical communication attacks, can be transformed into one that is secure against quantum communication attacks. Also notes that if the signal loss is high enough any QPV protocol can be attacked efficiently by a trivial teleportation attack.

On the equivalence between classically verifiable position verification and certified randomness (2026) 11 NOV

Presents a compiler that converts any single-device certified randomness protocol to classically verifiable position verification protocol which is secure in the random oracle model.

New lower bounds for CDS and $f$-routing (2026) 11 NOV

Gives linear lower bounds for perfect attacks for the inner product function.

Monogamy of highly symmetric states (2025) 11 NOV

The results in this paper imply a $\ln(2) \approx 0.69$ upper bound on the success probability of unentangled attacks.

Making existing quantum position verification protocols secure against arbitrary transmission loss (2025) 11 NOV

Introduces an extra commitment step into QPV and shows that for a class of protocols, involving $f$-BB84 QPV, this makes the transmission loss irrelevant for security. Argues $f$-BB84 is a practical QPV protocol.

Lossy-and-Constrained Extended Non-Local Games with Applications to Quantum Cryptography (2025) 11 NOV

Improved analyis of the BB84 protocol under loss compared to "Single-qubit loss-tolerant quantum position verification protocol secure against entangled attackers" (2023) by the same authors, introduces SDP hierarchies to compute bounds on lossy-and-constrained extended non-local games

Loss-Tolerant Quantum Position Verification for Metropolitan Area Networks (2026) 11 NOV

Introduces a QPV protocol that is fully loss-tolerant as long as all information travels at the speed of light and that has the prover prepare the quantum state. The protocol is secure in the random oracle model. It is demonstrated experimentally with off-the-shelf hardware.

Linear gate bounds against natural functions for position-verification (2025) 11 NOV

Provides a robust linear lower bound on the number of quantum gates/measurements needed to attack the inner product function.

I Prove, Therefore I Am: Spatiotemporal Multi-Party Computation (2026) 11 NOV

Uses prover’s ability to pass position verification as the definition of its physical presence at the asserted position.

How To Track Qubits Through Space and Time (Or: Sailing in a Quantum Boat) (2026) 11 NOV

Introduces a stronger notions of position verification called quantum localization, which requires that there is a specified, unclonable state at the verified spacetime point-and that this state can be found nowhere else. Security of such schemes in proven in the classical oracle model.

High-Fidelity Remote Graph State Preparation for Blind Quantum Computation (2026) 11 NOV

Proposes a QPV protocol based on remote graph state preparation.

Exponential lower bounds for low-degree strategies in position-based quantum cryptography (2026) 11 NOV

Proposes a variant of the phase unitary protocol that needs less resources for the honest parties, proves exponential lower bounds (i.e., exponential in all resources of the honest parties) against low-degree strategies. As in the preceding paper, there is also an exponential lower bound conditioned on an unresolved Banach space geometry conjecture.

Equivalence of non-local computation tasks beyond Clifford operations (2026) 11 NOV

Extends the equivalence between $f$-routing and $f$-measure (in the Hadamard basis) to $f$-measure with arbitrary bases, the controlled application of any Clifford unitary, and the controlled application of any unitary of the form $U=C_1 D C_0$ with $D$ an arbitrary diagonal unitary and $C_0$, $C_1$ Clifford circuits.

Enhancing Position Verification in Multi-Node Quantum Networks (2025) 11 NOV

Extension of QPV to 2D networks in a restricted attack model.

Efficient multi-basis quantum position verification secure against generalized adversaries (2026) 11 NOV

Improved analysis of how to use QPV to provide the authentication within QKD.

Continuous-variable quantum position verification secure against entangled attackers (2024) 11 NOV

Generalises $f$-BB84 QPV to continuous-variable inputs and shows analogous security statements as for finite-dimensional inputs.

Computational Bounds for $f$-Routing (2026) 11 NOV

Proves unconditional resource lower bounds for concrete $f$ in the case of uniform attackers under computational assumptions

Classical Verifier Position Verification from Non-Local Games (2026) 11 NOV

Provides a compiler that converts any complete-support non-local game with the required quantum advantage to a multi-prover classically verifiable position verification protocol.

Breaking the Bounded Entanglement Barrier for Quantum Position Verification (2026) 11 NOV

Introduces a model, in which the verifiers' messages are sent at a time drawn uniformly at random from a continuous time interval and proves security against LOCC adversaries. Also considers a model with discretized time where security depends on how accurately time can be measured

Arbitrarily Loss-Tolerant Quantum Position Verification in a Single Execution (2026) 11 NOV

Proves security of the $f$-measure protocol with commitment step (which is the version of the protocol that is fully loss tolerant) under parallel repetition.

A quantum cloning game with applications to quantum position verification (2025) 11 NOV

Proves security of the $f$-route protocol in the random oracle model by analyzing a quantum cloning game.

A complexity theory for non-local quantum computation (2026) 11 NOV

Finds relations between a wide range of different NLQC tasks, including showing that $f$-route and $f$-measure are equivalent, with a constant-overhead reduction between them.
